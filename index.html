01 · About

I keep backends boring to run.

Most small teams don't need another platform rewrite. They need the two or three things that will break first as they grow, found early and fixed properly: clear monitoring, safe rollouts, and a service nobody has to babysit.

That's the work I do at Lightspeed on an endpoint taking tens of millions of requests a week. I'm looking for a backend or platform role where that work matters, in Montréal or remote. I also take fixed-scope contract engagements alongside it.

Away from the terminal I run a YouTube channel, shoot street and portrait photography, and cook for anyone who'll sit down.

05 · How I work

Three habits

  1. 01

    Start in production

    Dashboards, incident history and the deploy pipeline before opinions. What breaks shows where to spend your money.

  2. 02

    Ship in safe slices

    Feature flags, canaries and shadow traffic. Every change is small enough to roll back in a minute.

  3. 03

    Leave the team stronger

    Runbooks, alerts and tests stay with you, so you don't need me on call after the engagement ends.

Also available for contract work

Separately from looking for a full-time role, I take fixed-scope engagements. Week one, I read your codebase, infrastructure and how you ship today. You get a short prioritised list of what will slow you down or break first as you grow, and what each fix costs. From there we pick what fits.
