01 — Why I'm reaching out

Senior backend help without a six-month hiring cycle.

Most small teams don't need another platform rewrite. They need the two or three things that will break first as they grow, found early and fixed properly — clear monitoring, safe rollouts, and a service nobody has to babysit.

04 — How I work

Three habits, no ceremony

  1. 01

    Start in production

    Dashboards, incident history and the deploy pipeline before opinions. What breaks shows where to spend your money.

  2. 02

    Ship in safe slices

    Feature flags, canaries and shadow traffic. Every change is small enough to roll back in a minute.

  3. 03

    Leave the team stronger

    Runbooks, alerts and tests stay with you, so you don't need me on call after the engagement ends.

How a first engagement goes

Week one, I read your codebase, infrastructure and how you ship today. You get a short prioritised list of what will slow you down or break first as you grow, and what each fix costs. From there we pick what fits.

1 week

Readiness audit

A read of the code, the infra and the pipeline, ending in a ranked list of risks with a cost against each one. Useful on its own even if we stop there.

2–4 weeks

Hardening sprint

We take the top items off that list and fix them: monitoring, alerting, safe rollouts, the query that falls over at 3× traffic. Runbooks included.

By feature

Extra senior engineer

You have a backend feature that needs shipping and no one free to ship it. I join the team, build it, document it and hand it over.
